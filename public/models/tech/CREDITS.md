# 3D model credits

The technology models shown around the emblem in the home hero. Files in this
folder are web-optimised copies (mesh compression, unused texture and
animation data removed) of the models listed below. At render time the
Solidity and Next.js models are recoloured for contrast on the dark
background and metallic finishes are softened; shapes are unchanged.
